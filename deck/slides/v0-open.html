This is the maths lecture that the VAE lecture stands on. Everything today is a toy: ten coin tosses, two bumps, a few shapes in a plane. Small enough to check every number by hand or on a 1-D grid.

The picture is a real run (scripts/make_figures_vi.py): stochastic gradient ascent on the ELBO with 10 draws per step, 400 steps. We will build it piece by piece and then press play on it live.
