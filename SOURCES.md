## Substitutions and qualifications

- StyleGAN mixing uses the inspected paper Figure 3 crop instead of the Google Drive figure file.
- Name generation uses a newly fitted, explicit 14-name character-bigram teaching model, not a claim of rerunning the earlier transformer. Seeds and categorical rule are visible.
- Fresh local VAE/GAN runs use procedural seven-segment binary glyphs, not MNIST. The published opening MNIST figures remain attributed to AEVB.
- The sleep traces are deterministic illustrative signals and a proposed task.
- Other research figures are attributed academic excerpts; their dataset photographs retain their original rights. The manifest does not assert a blanket licence for those photographs.

The local `attention-followups/beyond-generation/` lecture and rendered PDF were inspected directly, especially the shared-representation branching and training diagrams. The new figures use that teaching approach: a concrete input, a computation that visibly branches where the model branches, and separate forward and gradient paths. `src/figures.mjs` is the editable figure source; exported SVGs are in `figures/`.
